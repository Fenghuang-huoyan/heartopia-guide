Heartopia runs on a calendar. That is why a money guide from March is wrong in October even when every price on it was once true.

## What a season changes

- **Seeds.** Blanc’s list follows the season and your ranks. A crop table that ignores the season will tell you to buy something the shop is not selling.
- **Fish and bugs.** Piers and trees swap their rare spawns. The databases linked from [Fishing](/fishing) are the place to look up a specific shadow.
- **Forage and weather.** Rainbow weather and similar short skies are when gold guides tell you to walk. Treat them as a bonus route, not a scheduled raid. **Unverified timing.**
- **The town’s mood.** Festivals dress the square and add limited items. They do not pause DG requests. Clear the board anyway.

## How to prepare

1. Note the season end on the in-game calendar, not on a fan countdown.
2. The session before it, harvest anything you still need for recipes.
3. Turn in event objectives while the NPC is still offering them.
4. Redeem codes the same day. Event codes and season codes are the strings that die first. The hub is [Redeem codes](/redeem-codes).
5. Buy next season’s seeds after the change, in case the shop rotates overnight.

<Callout kind="warn" title="Not a live calendar">
Winter Frost, Dreamlight-style festivals, and similar names show up across 2026 guides. They are examples of the kind of event Heartopia runs. They are not a promise that one of them is active on the day you read this. If a page elsewhere lists today’s event crops, use it for the names, then confirm the board in game.
</Callout>
