Gold pays for seeds, tools, recipes, and the house. The efficient loop is short, and it is easy to skip the only step that matters: do not sell the raw ingredient when the dish exists.

## Selling rules

- Star quality raises the price. A 1-star tomato at the community base of 30 gold is not what a careful harvest is worth. Higher stars were only estimated on public tables, so this site does not print those guesses.
- Recipes and requests consume items. Sell the overflow, not the last of a request item.
- Community shops name **Albert Jr.** as a general buyer for fish, crops, and extras. If that NPC is not who your client uses, sell at whoever the interaction prompt offers. **Unverified name.**
- Codes sometimes include gold directly (the half-year bundles on old lists). That is a one-time mailbox payout, not a strategy. Current status is on [Redeem codes](/redeem-codes).

## Cook, then sell

Cooking is the margin. A jam, a meal, or a tea uses crops and fish you were about to vendor. Guides tell new accounts to unlock cooking with the first Hobby Expansion Ticket for this reason. The unlock itself is on [Cooking](/cooking).

Until Massimo is available, sell raw crops and fish and spend the gold on seeds you can actually harvest before the next session.

## Farming gold

Use the [crop table](/farming-seasons) as a timer, not as a stock tip.

- **Online hour:** tomatoes, then pineapple once the level allows. Fast turns.
- **Overnight:** wheat, strawberries, corn, or whatever Blanc will sell you that finishes near your next login.
- **Before a season change:** harvest and stop. Next season’s seeds are the purchase.

## Fishing and forage

Fishing is free money from the tutorial onward, capped by bait, time of day, and weather. The overview is on [Fishing](/fishing). It is a poor use of an hour if your request board is still full.

Forage is the other “while you walk” source. Gold guides single out **truffles**. Rainbow weather is the other repeated tip: a short window where rare nodes are supposed to be better. We have not timed that window. When the sky changes, walk the usual forage route before you settle into one fishing spot.

<Callout kind="note" title="What not to do">
Do not buy Wishing Stars to flip into gold. Do not empty your plots into the shop five minutes before a season change. Do not skip requests to “focus on money” — the requests are how the shops that sell better seeds open up.
</Callout>
