## How to redeem

You can redeem as soon as you control your character.

1. Open the watch menu. On PC, press **Tab** or click the watch icon. On a phone, tap the same icon.
2. Open **Settings**, then the **General** tab.
3. Choose **Redeem Code** (bottom-right on the layouts guides describe).
4. Paste the code. Capitalization has mattered on some strings, so paste rather than retype.
5. If the game accepts it, open the mailbox in front of your house and take the reward.

There is no redeem box on this website. Codes are entered in the game.

## When a code fails

- **Already redeemed.** The string can still be “active” for someone else.
- **Expired.** Campaign codes (180-day, June, summer, spring festival) die on a timer the trackers hear about late.
- **Region or account.** A code can be limited to new accounts or to one platform. The error text is often the same as a dead code.
- **Typo.** `0` and `O` show up in these strings. Paste.

New codes are posted by XD on the official social accounts linked in the footer (X `@myheartopia`, the official YouTube channel). Bookmark this page; the tables above are the part we update.

## What codes are actually for

Most published rewards are Wishing Stars, Moonlight Crystals, dyes, a bit of gold, fertilizer, or an emote. They skip a little early friction. They do not replace the daily resident requests that move your DG level. Spend the free stars on whatever the game already lets a new account buy, and read [Make gold](/make-money) before you sell the raw materials a code just handed you.
