Players search “DG level” because a shop is empty or a hobby is locked and the game does not say “come back tomorrow” in plain language. The rank is the explanation.

## What actually gives DG progress

Community guides line up on three sources:

- **Resident requests**, the daily board. Heartopia.Life describes five requests and a cap around 50 contribution medals a day.
- **Main story quests**, including whatever Atara, the mayor, is holding.
- **Playing hobbies**, which helps, and does not refill medals you left on yesterday’s board.

<Callout kind="warn" title="Unverified cap">
The “five requests / 50 medals” line is repeated, not copied from an XD patch note on this site. If your board shows a different number, trust the board.
</Callout>

## Early milestones

This is the part worth memorizing. It is not a level 50 chart.

| Milestone | What players report | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tutorial / DG 1 | Fishing, the house, basic tools, the request board | Repeated in beginner guides |
| DG 2 | Gardening with Blanc, seeds, Hobby Expansion Ticket #1, a couple of home plots | heartopiawiki.org writes this up as a researched page |
| Around DG 5 | The tutorial stops feeling like the whole game | Heartopia.Life tip, not a patch note |
| Cooking | Massimo, often after a ticket and a few more DG levels. One character guide says DG 6 plus a ticket | Sources disagree on the exact level |
| Later ranks | More shop stock, more plots, more hobbies, event access | Direction is stable; the per-level list is not maintained here |

Hobby **skill** and DG **rank** are different. A high gardening level can still be unable to buy a seed if the shop is locked behind DG. The carrot-seed example shows up on wiki tables as a DG gate. Treat any specific seed-at-level cell as Unverified until you see it in Blanc’s shop.

## How to spend the days

1. Clear the request board first, before a long hobby session.
2. Keep one stack of the items requests usually want: a common fish, a fast crop, a cooked dish once you have a recipe.
3. At DG 2, open gardening and plant something short if you will be online, something longer if you are about to log out. Details are on [Farming by season](/farming-seasons).
4. Hold the Hobby Expansion Ticket until you have read [Hobbies](/hobbies). The default gold answer is cooking. The honest answer is the hobby you will open tomorrow.

## What we are not publishing

Tidebell and the larger wikis keep dense DG and friendship tables. Copying those tables would be stale the week a patch adds a plot. If you need “what unlocks at level 27,” use those sites, then come back here for the daily rule: requests first, ticket on purpose, shop stock follows the rank.
