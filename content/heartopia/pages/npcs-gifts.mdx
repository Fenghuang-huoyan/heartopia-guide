You do not need every resident’s birthday to finish the first month. You need the three people who gate the loop, and a rule for gifts that does not depend on a wiki being right.

## People who change your week

| Name | Role in community guides | Why you care |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Atara | Mayor. Town and weekly-or-daily requests. | DG medals and the story |
| Blanc | Gardening mentor, seed shop. Map icon if the stall is empty. | Every crop on the farming page |
| Massimo | Cooking mentor. Recipes, ingredients, some furniture. | The cook-before-you-sell margin |
| Albert Jr. | Described as a general buyer for fish, crops, and extras | Where gold guides tell you to sell. **Unverified** if your client uses another shop |
| Vanya | Named on gift roundups | Role and favorites are not confirmed on this site. Use the in-game profile |
| Doris | A merchant some guides say appears in specific weather | Limited recipes and stock. **Unverified** schedule |

Shops for clothes, shoes, music, and puzzles (names that show up on character databases include Dorothee and Annie) are optional in week one. They are on the map when you want them.

## How gifting works

1. Open the villager and read liked and loved tags before you buy a flower.
2. Prefer a loved item, then a liked item, then conversation. A hated gift is a setback, not a neutral.
3. If they have a request, that item comes first. It may count for both the board and the friendship.
4. Stages increase over many gifts. One Crystal Flower will not max a villager. Full stage charts live on the dense wikis; we are not copying them.

<Callout kind="note" title="Crystal Flower">
Search interest around this flower is real, which usually means it is a strong gift for someone visible. It does not mean you should grow only that and hand it to the whole town. Grow a few hybrid flowers once Blanc’s flower beds make sense, and match them to the UI.
</Callout>

Player-to-player gifts and town visits are the other system. They are on [Friends and gifts](/friendship-gifts).
