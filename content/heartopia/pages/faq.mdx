## About the numbers

SteamCharts, checked for this site on 19 Oct 2026, showed an all-time peak of 65,894 and roughly 10,900 players online that day. The 30-day average on that chart was a bit under 8,000. Reviews were in Mixed territory. Those figures move. The store page itself is the source for “free,” “Windows,” and the publisher name XD.

The game’s store text is available in many languages. This wiki is English only.
